# 2026-10-09

**Do not "fix" a module checksum mismatch by changing `go.sum`.** During the
Tailscale v1.104.0 upgrade (#435), `go mod tidy` with the workstation's default
direct-first `GOPROXY` fetched `golang.zx2c4.com/wireguard/windows@v1.0.1`
with bytes that did not match the committed checksum. The public Go checksum
database confirmed the committed value; a fresh download through
`https://proxy.golang.org` matched it, and `go mod verify` succeeded. The
direct-origin discrepancy was not explained, so do not treat it as proof of a
compromise or silently accept its bytes. Keep `GOSUMDB` enabled and verify an
immutable proxy download against the public checksum before proceeding.
